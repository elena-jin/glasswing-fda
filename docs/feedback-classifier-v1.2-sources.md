# Feedback classifier boundary set v1.2 - sources and use

The combined JSON adds 35 records to the original 1,658 without altering any existing record or split: 8 exact public-source excerpts (2 train, 6 held-out eval), 27 authored adversarial cases (8 train, 19 held-out eval), including 10 two-record contrast pairs (4 train pairs, 6 held-out pairs). Total: 1,693 records, 1,312 train / 381 eval. The separate JSONL is **only the 6 real-source held-out eval records**, for testing without parsing the whole combined set. Each record includes source URL, original excerpt, provenance, expected route and rationale. Never use the held-out file for training, prompt tuning, threshold choice or error-driven iteration; maintain a separate dev set. A public medical-device report is an allegation, not a verified clinical outcome. The labels are draft routing examples for qualified Quality review, not regulatory determinations. FDA manufacturer narrative is staff paraphrase, not direct customer wording.

## Primary public sources actually used

- FDA MAUDE public narrative: [Abbott FreeStyle Libre 14 Day report](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfmaude/detail.cfm?mdrfoi__id=20653612&pc=PZE), [Dexcom G6 report](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfmaude/detail.cfm?mdrfoi__id=19630761&pc=QBJ). These narratives were directly read, and exact contiguous excerpts retained. Their own public redaction marker `(b)(6)` is preserved where present. Avoid copying header identifiers, ages, location or contacts into model input.
- [FreeStyle Libre 3 US public reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/freestyle-libre-3-us/id1524572429?see-all=reviews), [Dexcom Clarity public reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dexcom-clarity/id1019225730?platform=iphone&see-all=reviews), [Dexcom dark-mode forum post](https://www.reddit.com/r/dexcom/comments/1j42h5l/no_dark_mode/). Excerpts keep wording, typos and shorthand and omit handles. The reviews include real safe product ideas and alleged missed alarm/data loss; one mixed review cannot be marked wholly safe just because it contains a feature idea. These small excerpts are for internal evaluation only. Public visibility is not a bulk-training license; check each site's terms and rights before scaling, redistribution or external delivery. Do not crawl these sites at scale on the basis of this sample.

## Best next sources for a larger real-data test

1. **openFDA device adverse-event API and bulk downloads:** [device event overview](https://open.fda.gov/apis/device/event/), [bulk downloads](https://open.fda.gov/data/downloads/), [MAUDE dataset](https://open.fda.gov/data/maude/). Primary, machine-readable real complaint/malfunction narratives, many with reporter paraphrasing and incomplete facts. [License](https://open.fda.gov/license) says generally CC0/public domain unless noted, but third-party copyright, privacy/publicity, trademarks and GMDN licensing may remain; [terms](https://open.fda.gov/terms) govern use and limits. Filter by product code or device type, de-identify narratives again, use report keys for deduplication and temporal held-out tests. **This is complaint-heavy and cannot alone measure false positives against genuine product feedback.** FDA cautions MAUDE reports are incomplete, unverified and unsuitable for incidence rates or causal conclusions.
2. **Public app reviews / permitted first-party support exports:** app-store pages above supply authentic complaints and safe requests in the same source channel. For scale, have the app owner export reviews through their authorized tools or obtain rights/permission; [Apple developer review guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/ratings-and-reviews/) discusses owner-side viewing. Public reviews in this file do not establish an open redistribution or bulk training license. First-party support/CRM logs, if available, would best test real workflow distribution, but require lawful access, de-identification and a retained private provenance key.
3. **CGM social-language study repo:** [Continuous_Glucose_Monitoring_Sentiment_Analysis](https://github.com/sr9dc/Continuous_Glucose_Monitoring_Sentiment_Analysis) has MIT-licensed *repository code* and describes a third-party Excel social sentiment source. The repo license does not establish rights to that underlying review text; confirm its origin and availability before reuse. Sentiment is not the same label as complaint intake.
4. **Hugging Face commercial MAUDE preview:** [medical-device-safety-reasoning-preview](https://huggingface.co/datasets/NKODATALABS/medical-device-safety-reasoning-preview) advertises a small authentic preview, but its viewer failed in this check and the full corpus is a commercial *target*, not currently available. Its page states commercial terms start at $2,500; do not treat the preview or target as an open licensed dataset. Primary openFDA is the safer first route.
5. **Kaggle anonymized device-failure listing:** [listing](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/antoinepierreno/medical-device-failure-dataset-anonymized). The fetched listing did not expose a verifiable license or enough data provenance; do not use it as a real evaluation source without inspecting the actual file and rights. A title saying anonymized does not prove privacy safety or that the examples are genuine.

## Evaluation checks

- Preserve `provenance.kind`, `provenance.challenge_category`, source URL, exact excerpt, split and contrast pair ID. Original `paraphrased_public_source` records are **not** counted as new verbatim real examples.
- Run train-only for fit. Score both the old 356-record eval subset and the new 25-record challenge subset separately; report misses of `complaint`/Quality candidates first, then product-feedback false alarms and source-stratified counts. The six real eval cases are too few for a performance claim.
- Before a real rollout, assemble a larger separately permissioned, de-identified and human-reviewed real holdout with both labels and full-case context. Audit excerpts against entire source reviews to prevent safe-labeling a mixed complaint; labels here are provisional.
